01 — The Rules

How the Pool Works

Pari-mutuel betting — exactly how the real track does it. No house, no bookie, no fixed odds. The pot is split among the winners, weighted by what each person bet.

1

Everyone Chips In

Family and friends pick a horse and bet whatever they want. Every dollar goes into one pot. No house cut.

2

The Pool Sets the Odds

Heavy favorites pay small — the pool is split among many backers. Longshots pay huge because few people backed them. Real, honest odds emerge from the pool itself.

3

60 / 30 / 10

Backers of 1st split 60% of the pot. Backers of 2nd split 30%. Backers of 3rd split 10%. More people win something.

A Worked Example

Say the family pool reaches $400. You bet $20 on Renegade. Other backers put in $80, so the total bet on him is $100.

Now the race runs. Three things can happen.

If he wins

$48

You take 20% of the $240 win pool ($28 profit).

If he places (2nd)

$24

You take 20% of the $120 place pool ($4 profit).

If he shows (3rd)

$8

You take 20% of the $40 show pool (a small loss).

One bet. Three ways to win something. That's the magic of the 60/30/10.
